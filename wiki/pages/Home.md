# Mesh Barista

A three-node ESP32 system that pours measured amounts of "coffee", "milk" and "cream" (dyed water) over
an ESP-NOW mesh, with no router, cloud service or API. It is a model of a smart water distribution
system: three reservoirs are the source tanks and the cup is the destination tank. Each node that owns an
actuator makes its own safety decisions, so losing any one node never leaves a pump running.

ENGR 4399 · Cyber-Physical & IoT Systems · Project 2. Team: Sebastian Cadena, Daniel Critchlow Jr.
