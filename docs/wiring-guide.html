Before you start

Parts and ground rules

Build and test one node at a time. Unplug USB whenever you move a wire.

Node 1 · pump station

  • ESP32 DevKit
  • 3 relay modules
  • 3 pumps and tubing
  • 3 water sensors
  • 1 buzzer (3-pin)
  • Pump power supply
  • 3 diodes (1N4007)
  • 3 resistors, 10 kΩ (recommended)
  • 3 reservoir cups

Node 2 · control center

  • ESP32 DevKit
  • Joystick module
  • OLED, 0.96 in, 4-pin
  • 1 buzzer (3-pin)
  • USB cable to a laptop

Node 3 · output

  • ESP32 DevKit
  • 1 water sensor
  • OLED, 0.96 in, 4-pin
  • RGB LED, 4-pin (V G B R)
  • Output cup

Four rules for every node

Signal parts run on 3V3Water sensors, the joystick and the RGB LED go on the 3V3 pin, even where the part says +5V. More than 3.3 V into an ESP32 input can damage it.
Only relays use 5 VRelay modules take their power from the VIN (or 5V) pin.
All grounds joinEvery part's GND goes to an ESP32 GND pin. On Node 1 the pump supply's negative goes there too.
Pumps have their own supplyNever power a pump from an ESP32 pin. The relays switch the pump supply.

Reading the diagrams

Each line is one jumper wire from the ESP32 pin on the left to the part pin on the right. Pin names are GPIO numbers; most boards print them as D25, D32 and so on. Run 3V3 and GND to the breadboard's power rails first, so every part takes power from the rails.

MESH BARISTA · WIRING GUIDE04

Node 1 · step 2 of 2

Pump station: pumps and water

The pump side never touches an ESP32 pin except ground. Repeat this circuit for each of the three relays.

One pump circuit

  1. Pump supply + to the relay's COM terminal.
  2. The relay's NO terminal to the pump's + wire (usually red). Leave NC empty.
  3. The pump's − wire (usually black) to pump supply −.
  4. Pump supply − to an ESP32 GND pin. One wire covers all three pumps.
  5. A diode across each pump's two wires, with the striped end on the pump's + wire.
Check the diode directionA backwards diode shorts the supply the moment the relay closes. The relay clicks and the pump never runs.
Why NO and not NCOn NO, a pump only runs while the ESP32 is actively holding its relay on. A reset or power loss leaves every pump off.

Water and tubing

  • Each pump sits at the bottom of its reservoir. A submersible pump must be under water to pump.
  • Each water sensor is clipped inside its reservoir, strips pointing down, electronics at the top kept dry.
  • The three outlet tubes reach the cup on Node 3.
  • A tray sits under everything to catch spills.

Power-up

Checklist

Work down the list in order. Join the Wi-Fi the nodes create and open http://barista.io (or http://192.168.4.1).

  • Each node alone on USB. The OLED lights up on Nodes 2 and 3. Node 1 prints the dashboard address on Serial.
  • All three on. The Live tab shows three nodes ALIVE. The system starts in SAFE: press Acknowledge, or click the joystick.
  • Buzzers. Test tab: Beep Node 1 buzzer, then Beep Node 2 buzzer. Each beeps for one second.
  • LED. Cycle Node 3 LED shows red, then green, then blue.
  • Joystick. The Inputs card shows X and Y near 1900 at rest, moving toward 0 and 4095 when pushed.
  • Water sensors. Note each raw value dry and at the full mark. Put them in LEVEL_RAW_DRY / LEVEL_RAW_FULL on Node 1 and CUP_RAW_DRY / CUP_RAW_FULL on Node 3, then flash again.
  • Pumps, with the pump supply on. Coffee pump 10 mL, then milk, then cream. Each relay clicks and its pump runs briefly.
  • Flow rate. Pour 200 mL into a measuring cup and time it. Put mL divided by seconds into FLOW_ML_PER_S on Node 1, for each pump.
  • Fail-safe. Unplug Node 3 during a pour. The pump stops, both buzzers sound, and the dashboard names the output zone.

Troubleshooting

If something does not work

Find the symptom on the left. Causes are listed most likely first.

SymptomLikely cause
Relay clicks, pump does not runDiode backwards. Pump on NC instead of NO. Pump supply off or too weak. Pump not under water. Test the pump by touching its wires straight to the supply.
Relay clicks on at boot and off when a pour startsActive-LOW relay module. Set RELAY_ON to LOW on Node 1.
Relay does not click at allRelay VCC is not on VIN (5V), or the module will not switch from a 3.3 V signal.
Buzzer sounds all the timeActive-HIGH buzzer module. Set BUZZER_ON to HIGH in that node's sketch.
Level stays at 0 % or 100 %Sensor on the wrong pin, or the raw dry and full values are not calibrated yet.
OLED is blankSDA and SCL swapped, or a display that is not at address 0x3C.
Menu will not moveThe joystick was held during power-up, or VRx is not reaching GPIO 34. Check Joystick X on the Test tab.
LED colors are wrongTwo of the R, G, B wires are swapped. Run Cycle Node 3 LED to see which.
A node shows LOSTThat board is unpowered, or was flashed with a different MESH_SSID or MESH_CHANNEL.
barista.io will not loadNot joined to the nodes' Wi-Fi, mobile data is on, or http:// was left off. Use http://192.168.4.1 instead.
Stuck in SAFEAcknowledge is refused while Node 3 is LOST. Bring Node 3 back, then acknowledge.
